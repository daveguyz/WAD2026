How CampusPulse works

Finding out about campus events used to mean scrolling through dozens of WhatsApp groups and faded posters. Now it takes three steps.

  1. Create your free account

    Sign up with your NUST student number in under a minute.

  2. Discover and register

    Search and filter events, then reserve your seat with one click.

  3. Host your own event

    Clubs and societies submit events; admins review and publish them.
