About CampusPulse

A Campus Connect web application built by NUST students, for NUST students.

The problem

NUST hosts hundreds of activities every semester: guest lectures, career fairs, sports tournaments, club open days, wellness workshops and cultural celebrations. Yet many students only hear about them after they have happened.

Information is scattered across WhatsApp groups, notice boards, emails and social media. Organisers have no simple way to know how many people will attend, and students have no single place to see what is on.

Our solution

CampusPulse is a dynamic web application that centralises campus events. Students can:

  • browse and search all approved events by category, venue, date and price;
  • register (RSVP) for events with live capacity tracking;
  • submit their own club or society events for approval;
  • manage their bookings and events from a personal dashboard.

Administrators from Student Affairs moderate submissions and manage events, categories, venues, users and messages from a secure admin panel.

Frequently asked questions

Who can use CampusPulse?

Anyone can browse events. To register for events or host one, create a free account with your 9-digit NUST student number.

How do I host an event?

Log in and open Host an Event. Fill in the form and submit. An administrator reviews it, usually within one working day, and it then appears on the events page.

Can I cancel my registration?

Yes. Open the event or your dashboard and choose Cancel registration. Your seat is released for someone else.

Is my data safe?

Passwords are never stored in plain text; they are hashed with bcrypt. Sessions use secure HttpOnly cookies and all database queries are parameterised.
